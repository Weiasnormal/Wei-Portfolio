My team and I emerged as Finalists in my very first hackathon: the Batang Techno: Building the Minds of Tomorrow's Innovators challenge, hosted by the CICS Student Council at Batangas State University Alangilan.

## What we built

We built ResQLine, an end-to-end civic engagement and emergency management platform designed to address SDG 16 (Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions) by bringing transparency and accountability to government crisis response.

<Shot label="ResQLine citizen app and dispatcher dashboard screenshot" />

## My role

Taking on the roles of Team Leader, Pitcher, and Video Creator was an incredible experience that pushed me far out of my comfort zone. It challenged me to not only guide our technical vision, bridging a React Native citizen app with a real-time Next.js dispatcher dashboard, but also to translate our machine-learning triage system into a compelling, human-centric story for the judges.

<Shot label="Pitching on stage or pitch video still" />

## Key takeaways

- **The power of a clear narrative.** Building great software is only half the battle. Communicating why it matters and how it solves real-world problems is what truly drives impact.
- **Agile leadership under pressure.** Managing the development pipeline and leading an amazing group of developers through tight deadlines tested our limits and proved our resilience.
- **Tech for good.** Seeing how our code could practically empower everyday citizens and eliminate the "black hole" of government reporting was incredibly validating.

## Thanks

A massive shoutout to my entire team for the late nights, the relentless debugging, and the shared vision. We built something we can truly be proud of. Thank you as well to the organizers for hosting such a fantastic platform for young innovators.

Here's to building the minds of tomorrow, continuous learning, and to whatever challenge comes next.
