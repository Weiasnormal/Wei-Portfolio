## Three days to learn, five days to build

During the first week, we had a 3-day workshop focused on n8n and workflow automation. After that, we were given just 5 days to create our automation solution from scratch.

So we built an automated content creation and publishing workflow that connects Telegram, n8n, Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Facebook.

## How the workflow works

- **Telegram** is the starting point, where the user initiates the content creation process.
- **Google Sheets** stores the information about the product or service being promoted, including its details.
- **Google Drive** stores and manages the images used for the content.
- **n8n** is the automation backbone, processing the information and generating three different content variations based on a predefined personality and writing style.
- **Content selection** sends the generated options back to the user so they can choose which one to publish.
- **Facebook** is where the selected content is automatically published, completing the workflow.

## More than automating repetitive tasks

One of the things I enjoyed most about this project was that we weren't simply automating repetitive tasks. We were designing a workflow that could understand structured product information, generate content with a consistent personality, give users multiple options, and automate the publishing process.

And we built the entire solution using free tools, free APIs, and available free-tier resources.

## What I learned

You don't always need expensive tools to build something useful. Sometimes, the real challenge is figuring out how to connect the tools you already have.

Going from learning n8n in a 3-day workshop to building a working automation system within 5 days, and eventually placing 2nd Overall, was an experience I'm incredibly grateful for.
